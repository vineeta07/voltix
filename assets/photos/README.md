Rules

- Only real photos from the pilot: the meter, the smart plugs, the scooters
  and e-rickshaws, the charging bays.
- Anyone who can be recognised must have agreed to the photo being published.
  Record that in the Consent column (who agreed, and when).
- Avoid number plates and faces unless you have that consent.
- No AI-generated images.
- Landscape, at least 1600 px wide, JPEG or WebP.

Wanted shots: `meter.jpg` (the IoT meter on the supply), `plug.jpg` (a smart
plug on a charger), `bays.jpg` (vehicles charging), `rickshaw.jpg` (an
e-rickshaw on charge), `team.jpg` (optional).
